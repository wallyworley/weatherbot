# Review & changes — 2026-05-29 (Opus 4.8 session)

Third review in the 5/28–5/29 cycle. Prior docs: `audit_2026_05_28.md`,
`independent_review_2026_05_29.md`, `handoff_for_next_model_2026_05_29.md`.
Those two sessions diagnosed "10–18pp overconfidence" and reached for
calibration *mechanics* (widen factor, prior_n). This session validated the
magnitude but found the **shape** of the error points to a different root
cause and a different (cleaner) fix.

## Headline finding: the bot is adversely selected (winner's curse)

Overconfidence scales with how far the model diverges from the market mid
(all fills since 5/15, exit-independent, CLI settlement truth):

| distance from mkt mid | n | pred% | actual% | overconf |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <10pp | 166 | 49.7 | 42.2 | **+7.6** |
| 10–20pp | 110 | 59.8 | 41.8 | +18.0 |
| 20–35pp | 51 | 67.8 | 35.3 | **+32.5** |
| >35pp | 9 | 65.4 | 44.4 | +21.0 |

When the model agrees with the market it's ~calibrated; when it strongly
disagrees it's catastrophically wrong. On liquid weather contracts the market
is the better forecaster, and most of what the model scores as "edge" beyond
~20pp divergence is its own error. **This is the disease.** Calibration tuning
treats the symptom.

## Two methodological corrections to the prior sessions

1. **The held-only audit is selection-biased garbage.** Held-to-settlement
   win rates look like 80pp overconfidence — but take-profit (0.70) skims off
   everything that rallies, so the held cohort is the adversely-selected
   residual. The exit-included audit had the opposite bias (counts exits as
   wins). The TRUE curve uses settlement truth for *every* fill regardless of
   exit, and shows a flat **~+17pp** overconfidence above the 20% bucket while
   the 10–20% bucket is already calibrated (+1.5pp). The production calibrator
   (`_bucket_stats`) already measures the right thing (settlement truth, all
   signals) — only the human audits were contaminated.

2. **The error is location-shaped, not width-shaped.** A too-narrow
   distribution is calibrated at the median and errs only in the tails. A flat
   ~17pp gap across all buckets is a center/bias error. **Widening the
   distribution (the 1.40 bump) is the wrong lever** — it pulls probabilities
   toward 0.5, corrupting the already-calibrated low buckets while only
   partially helping the top. The per-bin empirical calibrator is the right
   lever because it can subtract ~17pp above 20% and leave the bottom alone.

## Strategic finding (not yet acted on)

Per-station realized P&L since the 5/26 expansion (3→19 stations) is **net
−$161** and noise-shaped (KBOS +124 / KSEA −105 on n≈20 each — that spread is
variance, not edge). Stations were graduated on *bias-table calibration
quality*, never on demonstrated trading edge. KNYC (+72) is the one cell with
a structural thesis. **Recommendation for the 6/02 audit:** contract back to
cells with a named, mechanistic reason to beat the market rather than spraying
19 cities and paying the fee twice on each. Held off this session because
(a) at n≈20/station it's mostly variance, (b) paper mode wants sample
velocity, (c) the divergence cap + calibrator changes below filter the bad
trades across all stations anyway, and (d) the 6/02 per-station audit is
purpose-built for this with more data.

kalshi_market → cli_obs`, compute `side_won` from CLI truth (NOT from
