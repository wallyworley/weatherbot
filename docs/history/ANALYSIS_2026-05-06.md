## Root Cause: Why P&L is Negative

Despite good win rate (50.6%), the bot is losing because:

1. **Forecast overconfidence** (primary)
   - Expected edge: $+675 (model thinks it's very confident)
   - Realized edge: -$357 (model is overconfident by ~$1,032)
   - The model's uncertainty is too small — confidence calibration is off

2. **May model drift** (secondary)
   - NBM forecasts warmer than May reality at KMDW (+3.5°F), KMIA (+2.0°F)
   - This is either seasonal shift or model degradation in spring transition
   - Bias correction is being applied, but model spread is too tight

3. **New station ramp** (tertiary, improving)
   - KMDW/KMIA graduation (May 2) created thin calibration initially
   - HFMETAR rollout (May 3) has improved KMDW accuracy 54% — positive signal
   - BIAS_GATE is correctly blocking thin data; fallback to April is working
